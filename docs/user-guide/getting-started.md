# Getting Started with Aegis

Welcome! This guide will help you set up Aegis for your team, even if you're not a tech person.

## What is Aegis?

Aegis is a **help desk** for your company. It helps you:

- 📝 **Track requests** - When someone needs help, they submit a ticket
- 📚 **Share knowledge** - Write guides so you don't answer the same question twice
- 💻 **Manage equipment** - Keep track of who has what laptop, phone, etc.
- 👥 **Organize contacts** - Store info about employees, vendors, and customers

---

## First-Time Setup (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Create Your Admin Account

1. Open Aegis in your browser (your IT person will give you the address)
2. You'll see a "Create Admin Account" screen
3. Enter your name, email, and choose a password
4. Click **Create Account**

🎉 You're now the admin!

### Step 2: Add Your Company Info

1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar
2. Click **General**
3. Fill in:
   - Company name
   - Your logo (optional)
   - Time zone
4. Click **Save**

### Step 3: Invite Your Team

1. Go to **Settings** → **Users & Roles**
2. Click **Invite User**
3. Enter their email address
4. Choose their role:
   - **Admin** - Can do everything (probably just you)
   - **Manager** - Can see all tickets and approve things
   - **Technician** - Handles tickets (your IT person)
   - **User** - Regular staff member
5. Click **Send Invite**

They'll get an email to set up their account!

## Understanding the Main Sections

### 🎫 Tickets

This is where help requests live.

**As an admin/manager:**
- See all tickets from everyone
- Assign tickets to the right person
- Track what's been resolved

**As a regular user:**
- Submit tickets when you need help
- See your own tickets and their status

### 📚 Knowledge Base

Your company's help library.

**Great for:**
- "How do I reset my password?"
- "What's the WiFi password?"
- "How do I submit expenses?"

Write it once, share the link forever!

### 💻 Assets

Track company equipment.

**Keep track of:**
- Laptops and computers
- Phones and tablets
- Monitors and accessories
- Software licenses

Know who has what, when it was purchased, and when warranties expire.

### 👥 Contacts

Your people directory.

**Store info about:**
- Employees (name, email, department, manager)
- Vendors (your software providers, IT support)
- Customers (if you provide support to external people)

## Daily Tasks

### Checking New Tickets

1. Click **Tickets** in the sidebar
2. Look at the **New** column
3. Click a ticket to read it
4. Either:
   - **Reply** with an answer
   - **Assign** to someone else
   - **Change status** (In Progress, Waiting, Resolved)

### Writing a KB Article

Someone asked a question you've answered before? Write an article!

1. Go to **Knowledge Base**
2. Click **New Article**
3. Pick a folder (Getting Started, How-To, etc.)
4. Write your guide
5. Click **Save**

Next time someone asks, just send them the link!

### Adding a New Employee

1. Go to **Contacts**
2. Click **New Contact**
3. Fill in their info
4. Click **Save**

Then invite them to Aegis (Settings → Users → Invite)

## Tips for Small Teams

### You Don't Need Everything!

Start with just:
1. **Tickets** - For help requests
2. **Knowledge Base** - For common questions

Add Assets and other features later when you need them.

### Keep It Simple

- Don't create too many ticket categories (3-5 is plenty)
- Don't overthink permissions (everyone can see everything in a small team)
- Write KB articles as questions come up, not all at once

### Let Everyone Help

In **Settings → Knowledge Base**, turn on **"Anyone can publish"**. 

This lets your whole team contribute knowledge, not just the admin!

## Common Questions

### "Someone left the company. What do I do?"

1. Go to **Contacts** → Find their profile
2. Click **Deactivate** (don't delete - you'll lose history)
3. Go to **Settings** → **Users** → Remove their login access

### "I need to track something that doesn't fit anywhere"

Use **Custom Fields**! Go to Settings → Custom Fields and add your own fields to tickets, assets, or contacts.

### "Can customers submit tickets?"

Yes! Create a **Customer Portal** link (Settings → Portal) and share it with customers. They can submit and track tickets without seeing internal stuff.

### "How do I back up our data?"

Aegis automatically backs up daily. For extra safety, go to Settings → Backups to download a copy.
