# Knowledge Base - User Guide

## What is the Knowledge Base?

The Knowledge Base (KB) is where you store helpful articles, guides, and answers to common questions. Instead of answering the same question over and over, write it once and share the link!

## Who Can Use It?

**Everyone can read articles** (based on permissions set by your admin).

**Anyone can write articles** if your admin enables it. By default:
- Admins can create and publish articles
- Team members can suggest articles (admin approves)
- Or your admin can let everyone publish directly (great for small teams!)

---

## Creating Your First Article

### Step 1: Go to Knowledge Base
Click **Knowledge Base** in the left sidebar, then click **New Article**.

### Step 2: Choose a Folder
Pick where your article belongs:
- **Getting Started** - For new employee guides
- **How-To Guides** - Step-by-step instructions
- **Troubleshooting** - Common problems and solutions
- **FAQs** - Quick answers to frequent questions
- **Policies** - Company rules and procedures

💡 **Tip:** The folder you choose determines who can see the article!

### Step 3: Write Your Article

**Title:** Make it clear and searchable
- ✅ Good: "How to Reset Your Password"
- ❌ Bad: "Password stuff"

**Summary:** One sentence describing what this article covers

**Content:** Write your guide! You can:
- Add headings (## Like This)
- Make bullet lists
- Add numbered steps
- Include links
- Bold **important** text

### Step 4: Save
Click **Save** and you're done! 

For small teams, articles are published immediately. Larger organizations may require approval first.

## Organizing Articles

### Folders (Categories)

Think of folders like filing cabinets. Each folder can have different visibility:

| Folder | Who Can See |
|--------|-------------|
| 📁 Public FAQs | Anyone (even without logging in) |
| 📁 Employee Guides | Anyone logged in |
| 📁 HR Policies | HR team only |
| 📁 IT Documentation | IT team only |
| 📁 Management | Managers only |

### Creating a New Folder

1. Go to **Settings** → **Knowledge Base**
2. Click **New Folder**
3. Name it and choose who can see it:
   - **Everyone** - All logged-in users
   - **Specific Teams** - Pick which teams
   - **Public** - Anyone, no login needed

## Finding Articles

### Search
Type in the search box at the top of the Knowledge Base. It searches:
- Article titles
- Article content
- Tags

### Browse by Folder
Click any folder to see all articles inside.

### From a Ticket
When viewing a ticket, related KB articles appear in the sidebar. Click to read!

## Tips for Writing Great Articles

### 1. Answer One Question
Each article should answer ONE question clearly.

**Good:** "How to Connect to VPN"
**Bad:** "VPN, Email, and Printer Setup" (split into 3 articles!)

### 2. Use Simple Language
Write like you're explaining to a friend, not a computer.

**Good:** "Click the blue button that says 'Submit'"
**Bad:** "Execute the form submission action via the primary CTA"

### 3. Add Screenshots
A picture is worth a thousand words! Add screenshots for complex steps.

### 4. Keep It Updated
Set a reminder to review articles every few months. Outdated info is worse than no info!

### 5. Link Related Articles
At the bottom, link to related articles: "See also: How to Reset Your Password"

## Common Questions

### Can I edit someone else's article?
- **Small teams:** Usually yes! Anyone can improve articles.
- **Larger teams:** You may need to suggest changes for approval.

### How do I delete an article?
Click **Edit** on the article, then **Delete** at the bottom. Only admins can delete.

### Can customers see our KB?
Only if you put articles in a **Public** folder. Internal folders are hidden from customers.

### How do I know if my article is helpful?
Each article shows:
- 👁️ View count (how many people read it)
- 👍 Helpful votes (did it answer their question?)

Low helpful votes? Time to improve the article!

## For Admins: Quick Setup

### Small Team (Under 10 people)
1. Go to **Settings** → **Knowledge Base**
2. Enable **"Anyone can publish"** 
3. Create a few folders (Getting Started, How-To, FAQs)
4. Done! Your team can start writing.

### Larger Team (10+ people)
1. Keep approval workflow enabled
2. Assign **KB Managers** who can approve articles
3. Create team-specific folders with restricted access
4. Consider adding **Contributors** (subject matter experts)
