## Keyboard Shortcuts

|----------|--------|

| `R` | Reply to ticket |

## Ticket Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 🔵 **New** | Just submitted, no one's looked at it |
| 🟡 **In Progress** | Someone's working on it |
| 🟠 **Waiting** | Waiting for info from the requester |
| 🟢 **Resolved** | Fixed! Waiting for confirmation |
| ⚫ **Closed** | Done and dusted |

---

## Priority Levels

| Priority | Response Time | Use For |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| 🔴 **Critical** | ASAP | System down, everyone affected |
| 🟠 **High** | Same day | Can't work, blocking issue |
| 🟡 **Medium** | 1-2 days | Important but can wait |
| 🟢 **Low** | This week | Nice to have, not urgent |

## Common Tasks

### Submit a Ticket
1. Click **Tickets** → **New Ticket**
2. Fill in subject and description
3. Pick a category
4. Click **Submit**

### Find an Article
1. Click **Knowledge Base**
2. Type in the search box
3. Click the article to read

### Check Equipment
1. Click **Assets**
2. Search by name, tag, or person
3. Click to see details

### Look Up Someone
1. Click **Contacts**
2. Search by name or email
3. Click to see their info

## Tips

✅ **Be specific** in ticket subjects
- Good: "Can't print to HP LaserJet in Room 204"
- Bad: "Printer broken"

✅ **Include screenshots** when possible

✅ **Check the KB first** - your answer might already be there!

✅ **Update your tickets** - if you figured it out, let us know!
